// 01 — the tryb loop

Meet. Play. Belong.

Belonging comes from doing things together, not from scrolling past each other. Every connection on Tryb starts with something to do.

01

Meet

Pick your college and you're instantly in your campus Tryb. Discover people through what they play, not what they post.

02

Play

Jump into real-time matches, party games and daily challenges. Short, low-pressure, and way more fun than "hey".

03

Belong

Rivalries become rituals. Rituals become your Tryb — with leagues, elections, chat and an inbox that's a deck, not a feed.
